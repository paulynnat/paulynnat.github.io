About

My work started in the classroom. I wrote exam writing guides for Singapore students and the General Paper guide Writing the World, and I built small tools like Phonics Sonics to test how children learn to read. Building things is how I find out whether an idea really helps a learner. Digital Literacy Studio is where that work lives now.
