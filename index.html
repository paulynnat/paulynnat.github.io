Sources

  • Oregon, 2008: bans non-competes for hourly workers.
  • California: banned throughout. Cal. Bus. & Prof. Code 16600.
  • United States federal: an FTC rule in April 2024 would ban non-competes for most workers. A court set the rule aside and the FTC dropped its appeal in September 2025. FTC press release, September 2025.
  • United Kingdom: in 2023 the government proposed a 3 month cap. It was not passed, so the law stays case by case. The dashed stone marks the proposal. UK government response on non-compete clauses (PDF).
  • Germany: limits throughout. The maximum is 24 months, and the employer pays at least 50% of the last salary.
  • Singapore: case by case throughout. In 2023 MOM said employers should avoid these clauses for lower-paying jobs, and promised guidelines. The guidelines had not been issued by March 2026. MOM written answer, 21 April 2023. MOM written answer, 8 January 2025.
